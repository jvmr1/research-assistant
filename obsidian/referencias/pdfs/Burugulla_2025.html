Decentralized Identity Systems for Secure Public Engagement Advancing Inclusive Access and Privacy through Blockchain Driven Verifiable Credentials and Zero Knowledge Proofs

In Proceedings of the 1st International Conference on Intelligent Methods and Advanced Computer Scientific Innovations - Volume 4: IMACSI, 463-469, 2025 , NAMAKKAL & KAKKINADA, India

Authors: Jai Kiran Reddy Burugulla 1 ; Kishore Challa 2 ; Jayareka K S 3 ; Syed Zahidur Rashid 4 ; Kamred Udham Singh 5 and Senthil Kumar S 6

Affiliations: 1 Senior Engineer, American Express, Phoenix, U.S.A. ; 2 Lead Software Engineer, Mastercard International INC, O'Fallon, MO 63368, U.S.A. ; 3 Department of CSE, Sona College of Technology, Salem, Tamil Nadu, India ; 4 Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, International Islamic University Chittagong, Chittagong, Bangladesh ; 5 School of Computing, Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun, & Graphic Era Deemed to be University, Dehradun, Uttarakhand‑248002, India ; 6 Department of EEE, New Prince Shri Bhavani College of Engineering and Technology, Chennai‑600073, Tamil Nadu, India

Keyword(s): Decentralized Identity, Blockchain, Verifiable Credentials, Zero-Knowledge Proofs, Scalability, Privacy, Inclusivity

Abstract: But decentralized identity systems are revolutionizing these governments– public engagement ensuring services protected by a secure, privacy-ensuring and inclusive access. In this research paper, we investigate how combining blockchain based verifiable credentials with zero knowledge proofs (ZKPs) can help solve some of the main bottleneck problems, such as: scalability, interoperability and regulatory compliance. These systems allow for performant, user-centric designs that work for a wide range of demographics with a high level of performance and data minimization standards; making use of highly innovative, leading-edge privacy-preserving technologies. Furthermore, the paper also focuses on domain-specific use cases and applications within the intelligent community, smart cities, e-governance and IoT ecosystem, establishing a baseline for expanded implementation and practical use. In summary, this work lays the foundation for both theoretical and practical knowledge about decentral ized identity systems, catalysing innovation in secure public engagement and digital inclusion abroad and at home. (More)

But decentralized identity systems are revolutionizing these governments– public engagement ensuring services protected by a secure, privacy-ensuring and inclusive access. In this research paper, we investigate how combining blockchain based verifiable credentials with zero knowledge proofs (ZKPs) can help solve some of the main bottleneck problems, such as: scalability, interoperability and regulatory compliance. These systems allow for performant, user-centric designs that work for a wide range of demographics with a high level of performance and data minimization standards; making use of highly innovative, leading-edge privacy-preserving technologies. Furthermore, the paper also focuses on domain-specific use cases and applications within the intelligent community, smart cities, e-governance and IoT ecosystem, establishing a baseline for expanded implementation and practical use. In summary, this work lays the foundation for both theoretical and practical knowledge about decentralized identity systems, catalysing innovation in secure public engagement and digital inclusion abroad and at home.
